# Event content and artwork

Event date, timing, category fees, benefits and policy baseline were supplied by the organiser for reuse from [the central event website](https://www.sekhonmarathon.com/). Suratgarh, Desert Braves and the restricted audience are organiser-provided changes. Route maps and real QR payment details remain unconfigured. The organiser supplied contact numbers 8838463776 and 7027964880.

The shared Sekhon event logo is from [the reference site](https://www.sekhonmarathon.com/). The Gwalior wing crest, Gwalior-labelled artwork and payment QR are not used.

The Rajasthan background is original AI-generated canvas-style artwork. It is an illustration, not an event photograph or a depiction of the actual Suratgarh route. It was inspired by:

- [PIB, Sekhon IAF Marathon, 2 November 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2185553&lang=2&reg=48)
- [HAL Jaguar reference](https://www.hal-india.co.in/product/jaguar)
- [PIB Jaguar photograph](https://www.pib.gov.in/ShowAlbum.aspx?albumid=160916)
- [Ministry of Defence Mi-17V5 induction](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/PrintRelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=80383)
- [IAF Mi-35 photograph mirrored on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MI-35.JPG)

The final image is served as a 315 KB WebP to reduce mobile page weight.

## Mobile and legacy improvements, 5 September 2026

`desert-braves-mobile.webp` is a new original AI-generated canvas illustration derived from the inspected desktop artwork. Its 1200 × 900 composition keeps the sun, runners, Jaguar, Mi-17 V5 and Mi-35 together at the natural 4:3 ratio. The HTML text sits outside the painting on phones. The 208 KB image is decorative; it does not depict an approved race route or promise a flypast.

`sekhon-2025-legacy.webp` is a genuine photograph from the Ministry of Defence / PIB release [Sekhon IAF Marathon, 2 November 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2185553&lang=2&reg=48). It shows runners at the inaugural event in Delhi, not Suratgarh 2026. The page visibly credits the year, location, Ministry of Defence and PIB. [Original photograph](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/userfiles/image/WhatsAppImage2025-11-02at5.12.20PM%281%29XLTG.jpeg). The [PIB reproduction policy](https://www.pib.gov.in/content/132_2_Copyright-Policy.aspx?lang=1&reg=1) permits accurate reproduction with prominent acknowledgement, except third-party material; the release has no separate third-party credit for this image. The WebP is 1600 × 962 and about 159 KB, with its full frame preserved.

The original logo is retained as source; an optimised WebP is used in the page for faster loading at its small displayed size.
